# Stack - Organize Your Life, Beautifully 📋

Stack is a minimalist, modern, and efficient shopping list application built with **Jetpack Compose**. It focuses on simplicity and speed, allowing you to manage your household needs without any clutter.

## ✨ Features

- **Sleek & Minimalist UI:** Designed with a soft pink aesthetic and intuitive controls.
- **Google Sign-In:** Secure and fast authentication.
- **Real-time Synchronization:** Powered by Firebase Firestore, your lists are synced across all your devices instantly.
- **Cloud-Based Storage:** Never lose your data; everything is safely stored in the cloud.
- **Ad-Supported:** Integrated with Google AdMob to keep the service free.

## 🛠 Tech Stack

- **Language:** [Kotlin](https://kotlinlang.org/)
- **UI Framework:** [Jetpack Compose](https://developer.android.com/jetpack/compose)
- **Backend:** [Firebase Authentication](https://firebase.google.com/docs/auth) & [Cloud Firestore](https://firebase.google.com/docs/firestore)
- **Monetization:** [Google AdMob](https://admob.google.com/home/)
- **Architecture:** MVVM (Model-View-ViewModel)
